Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information FedEthics Inc. collects through this website, why we collect it, who processes it, and how you can access or delete your data.

Last updated: August 5th, 2026

Who we are

FedEthics Inc. ("FedEthics", "we", "us") operates the website fedethics.ca. If you have any questions about this policy or your personal data, contact us at hello-bonjour@fedethics.ca.

What data we collect and why

We only collect personal information that you actively provide, plus limited technical analytics data. We do not sell your personal information.

DataWhen it is collectedWhy we collect it
Your email address When you subscribe to the FedEthics Dispatch newsletter To send you the newsletter (AI governance insights, book updates, and speaking announcements). You can unsubscribe at any time.
Usage & device data (pages viewed, approximate location, browser type, referring links) Automatically, via cookies, once you accept analytics cookies To understand how the site is used so we can improve it. See "Cookies and analytics" below.
Payment details When you buy a book Payments are processed entirely by Stripe. FedEthics never receives or stores your full card details.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to measure how visitors use the site. These tools set cookies only after you give consent through the cookie banner shown on your first visit. Until you accept, analytics run in a consent-denied mode that does not store identifying cookies.

You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's cookies in your browser, which will make the consent banner appear again on your next visit.

Third parties who process your data

  • Google (Analytics & Tag Manager) — website usage measurement. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe — secure payment processing for book orders. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Newsletter delivery — your email is stored solely to operate the FedEthics Dispatch newsletter and is not shared for any other purpose.

How long we keep your data

We keep your newsletter email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' standard retention settings. Payment records held by Stripe are governed by Stripe's own policies.

How to access or delete your data

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To do so:

  • Newsletter: click "unsubscribe" in any newsletter email, or email us at hello-bonjour@fedethics.ca and we will remove your address.
  • Any other data: email hello-bonjour@fedethics.ca with your request. We will respond within 72 hours.

Because we operate from Canada, we handle personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Email hello-bonjour@fedethics.ca.
